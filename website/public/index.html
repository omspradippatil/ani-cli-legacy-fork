Frequently Asked Questions

Is Om CLI anime streaming completely free?

Yes! Om CLI is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license with zero advertisements, popups, or paid paywalls.

Does Om CLI support both Japanese Sub and English Dub?

Yes, Om CLI features a universal audio switcher that lets you switch between Japanese with English subtitles (SUB) and English dubbed audio (DUB) with a single click, syncing seamlessly across web and TV.

How do I install the Om CLI Android TV app?

You can download the production-signed APK directly from the Om CLI website or GitHub repository, transfer it to your Android TV, Fire TV, or Google TV device, and install it with full D-pad remote support.

Does Om CLI sync watch progress between Web and TV?

Yes! Enter the 6-character TV pairing code displayed on your Android TV screen into the web Link TV modal, and your watch history, continue watching queue, and anime watchlist will sync automatically in real-time.
